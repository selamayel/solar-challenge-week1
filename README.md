"# solar-challenge-week1" 
This project is part of the 10 Academy Week 0 Challenge and focuses on exploratory data analysis (EDA) of solar radiation data from Benin, Sierra Leone, and Togo. The goal was to identify patterns in solar irradiance, clean and analyze the data, and compare solar potential across countries.

What’s Included:
-EDA notebooks for each country under /notebooks
-Cleaned datasets under /data (ignored by Git)
-A cross-country comparison notebook (compare_countries.ipynb)
-Visualizations including time series, boxplots, heatmaps, and scatter plots
-GitHub branches organized by task (eda-benin, eda-sierra_leone, eda-togo, compare-countries)

Key Insight:
Benin showed the strongest overall solar potential based on higher GHI and DNI averages. Togo followed with stable irradiance trends, while Sierra Leone presented lower values and greater data inconsistencies.
